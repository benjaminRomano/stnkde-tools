ANNUAL COLLISION DENSITY — 2017–2026

The annual grid covers full calendar years 2017–2025 and partial 2026 through June 11. The same-period grid restricts every year to January 1–June 11. Neither grid annualizes partial data.

All maps share one logarithmic log1p scale, one fixed modern road network, and the slightly closer Manhattan framing (1.15x). Each year uses exact nearest-road assignments accepted within 100 m and the corrected spatial Epanechnikov estimator with 100 m bandwidth and 50 m lixels. There is no temporal smoothing between years. Only Manhattan borough-labeled event records are included; Queens roads provide context.

Recorded collision concentration is not per-trip crash risk. COVID-era traffic disruption, reporting changes, missing coordinates, and unmodeled road changes limit trend interpretation. The source reports a paused update process; its latest records are June 11, 2026.

The repository includes grid previews and historical methodology receipts. Individual 4800 x 7800, 300 PPI panels can be regenerated with poster/render.py. The frozen data snapshot, assignment quality, counts and density fields are in poster/annual/data/.
